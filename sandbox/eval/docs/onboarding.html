Welcome to Northwind

This document serves to inform newly onboarded personnel of the policies, procedures and expectations that are applicable during the initial period of employment. Personnel are advised to review its contents in full prior to their commencement date.

Before your first day

Your manager sends you a calendar invite for your first morning. Bring a photo ID, because the front desk prints your badge on arrival.

Your first week

On Monday you meet your manager and your onboarding buddy, who is a teammate that answers your questions during the first month. You also pick up your laptop from the IT desk on the second floor, where someone helps you sign in for the first time and set up two-factor authentication on your phone. On Tuesday and Wednesday you attend the new-hire sessions, which cover how the company makes money, how teams plan their work, and the security rules that everyone must follow. These sessions start at 9:30 and end before lunch, and lunch is provided on both days in the large kitchen. On Thursday you shadow a teammate for the whole day to see how the team works in practice. On Friday you have a short check-in with your manager to talk about how the week went and to agree on your goals for the first 30 days. Throughout the week, do not worry if you feel lost, because everyone does at first, and your buddy is there to help.
