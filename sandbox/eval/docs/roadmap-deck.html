Q3 roadmap

Three bets for the next quarter, and what we stop doing.

Product team · Jul 1, 2026

We will focus the whole of the third quarter on three product bets that our customers asked for most often

  • Offline mode for the mobile app.
  • Shared folders with permissions.
  • A faster search that also finds text inside attachments.

What we stop doing.

  • The desktop widget, which 2 percent of people use.
  • Custom themes.
